IT Calendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

IT Calendar is a personal task-calendar application that runs locally on the owner's own Windows computer. It is used internally by its owner and is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

When you connect your Google account, the app requests only these permissions:

  • Calendars created by this app (calendar.app.created) — to create one calendar named “IT Calendar” and to create, update and delete events in that calendar only. The app cannot see or change any of your other calendars.
  • Your email address (openid email) — only to show which Google account is connected.

How data is used

Tasks you enter in the app (title, details, date, time, category, assignee, reminder settings) are sent to your “IT Calendar” in Google Calendar so that Google can send you reminders on your phone, computer and by email. Data is used for no other purpose.

Storage

Task data and the Google authorization token are stored only in a local database file on your own computer. There is no external server operated by the app. Data sent to Google Calendar is stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI/ML models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • In the app: Settings (⚙) → “Disconnect” revokes the app's access to your Google account.
  • Anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete the “IT Calendar” calendar in Google Calendar, and delete the local database file data/tasks.db to remove all local data.
